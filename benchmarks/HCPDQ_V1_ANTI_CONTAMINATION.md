# HCPDQ-v1 Anti-Contamination Declaration

- Calibration cases are expendable and do not certify comparative performance.
- Held-out cases remain unavailable to productive tuning after freeze.
- Ground truth, rubrics, human answers, and expected outcomes never enter the subject payload.
- Benchmark results do not write Memory/Learning, modify thresholds, grant authority, or apply self-improvement.
- A material production change after held-out exposure invalidates comparative certification for that frozen corpus.
