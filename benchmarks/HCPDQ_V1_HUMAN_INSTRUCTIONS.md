# HCPDQ-v1 Human Reference Instructions

Participants receive only the case prompt and neutral response instructions.
They do not receive ROBY outputs, answer keys, expected outcomes, rubrics, or other participants' answers.
Timing starts when the full prompt is presented and stops at final submission.
One scored attempt is permitted per held-out case. External tools are disallowed unless a later study variant explicitly permits them.
Participants may answer `abstain` when the supplied evidence is insufficient.
