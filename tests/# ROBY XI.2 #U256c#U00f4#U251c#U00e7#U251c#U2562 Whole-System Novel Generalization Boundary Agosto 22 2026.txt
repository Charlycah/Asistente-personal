# ROBY XI.2 — Whole-System Novel Generalization Boundary

## Contract / Go-No-Go

**Stage:** Contract / Go-No-Go
**Production state:** **FROZEN**
**Official baseline:** **12,760 PASS · 0 FAIL · Ruff clean**
**Previous determination:** **0 Genuine Missing Mechanisms demonstrated**
**Purpose:** determine whether ROBY's existing governed cognitive authorities can compose learned structural knowledge in genuinely novel but structurally compatible contexts.

---

# 1. Architectural question

This stage answers one precise question:

> **Can ROBY reuse previously learned structural knowledge in a context whose surface vocabulary, subject matter or concrete entities are genuinely different, while the relevant underlying structure remains compatible, using only existing authorities and preserving governance?**

The test is **not** whether ROBY has unrestricted general intelligence.

It is also not whether arbitrary similarity can cause transfer.

The intended capability is:

**learned structural knowledge**
→ **novel context**
→ **structural compatibility detection**
→ **semantic/cognitive selection**
→ **governed transfer**
→ **bounded composition**
→ **reviewable reasoning result**

without creating a new cognitive authority.

---

# 2. Existing-authority hypothesis

The current architecture already contains the likely complete authority chain:

**Cognitive Memory / Learned Knowledge**
↓
**Contextual Grounding**
↓
**Governed Structural Knowledge Transfer**
↓
**Transfer Instantiation / Admission Guard**
↓
**Semantic Selection & Composition — `CognitivePlanner`**
↓
**Governed Reasoning**
↓
**Mental Model / Reflection / Planning**
↓
**Canonical decision path where applicable**

Therefore the contract begins with this hypothesis:

> **Novel generalization should emerge from composition of existing authorities rather than from a new “generalization engine.”**

### Forbidden interpretation

A RED test must **not** be taken automatically as justification for:

* new generalization manager;
* new semantic classifier;
* new analogy engine;
* new orchestrator;
* new memory authority;
* new planner;
* new autonomous learning system.

A RED result first indicates only:

> **the existing composition failed the contract.**

Its cause must be determined separately.

---

# 3. Definition of “novel context”

A test qualifies as **novel** only when the target context differs materially from the learned/source context in surface representation.

At least two of these should differ:

* domain vocabulary;
* entity names;
* concrete objects;
* narrative framing;
* sentence construction;
* incidental details;
* target task wording.

But the relevant abstract structure must remain compatible.

Example conceptual pattern:

**Source learned structure**

> A resource with limited capacity must be allocated among competing demands while preserving a mandatory reserve.

Possible novel target:

> scheduling available laboratory time across experiments while keeping mandatory calibration capacity.

The surface domain changes.

The structural relation does not.

The contract is therefore testing **structural reuse**, not keyword overlap.

---

# 4. Positive contract

ROBY should be capable of producing a governed transfer when all of the following hold:

1. relevant source knowledge has actually been acquired or provided;
2. source knowledge is grounded;
3. target context is grounded sufficiently;
4. a structurally relevant mapping exists;
5. the mapping preserves role relations;
6. owner/scope rules remain compatible;
7. provenance remains traceable;
8. ambiguity is below the admission threshold;
9. no negation or quoted-span protection invalidates the candidate;
10. transfer does not create new authority;
11. transferred knowledge remains subject to existing review/admission rules;
12. downstream composition uses existing cognitive authorities.

Expected outcome:

# **TRANSFER / COMPOSITION AVAILABLE UNDER GOVERNED BOUNDARIES**

not:

**automatic truth promotion**.

---

# 5. Negative contract

ROBY must refuse, abstain or require review when structural justification is insufficient.

The following must **not** trigger confident generalization merely because vocabulary looks related.

### Surface similarity without structural compatibility

Same words or domain, different causal/relational structure.

Expected:

**NO TRANSFER**

---

### Structural ambiguity

Two or more learned structures fit the target with insufficient evidence to prefer one.

Expected:

**AMBIGUOUS / REVIEW REQUIRED**

---

### Scope mismatch

The learned rule belongs to an incompatible scope.

Expected:

**NO TRANSFER**

---

### Ungrounded target

The target lacks enough evidence to establish the structural roles required for transfer.

Expected:

**NO TRANSFER / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**

---

### Negated evidence

A relevant relation appears only inside a negated proposition.

Expected:

**must not be promoted into affirmative evidence**

---

### Quoted span

A structurally relevant phrase appears only as quoted/reported material and is not asserted by the current context.

Expected:

**must not become active target evidence merely by lexical presence**

---

### Distractor similarity

The target contains high-overlap vocabulary unrelated to the operative structure.

Expected:

**distractor must not win selection**

---

### Provenance corruption

The transferred structure lacks valid source/provenance correspondence.

Expected:

**REJECT**

---

# 6. Novelty families to certify

The RED package should not depend on one phrase family.

It should include multiple kinds of novelty.

## N1 — Domain shift

Same abstract structure, different real-world domain.

Example classes:

* logistics → scheduling;
* maintenance → household organization;
* resource allocation → project planning;
* queue management → task prioritization.

Purpose:

**verify structural transfer beyond source-domain vocabulary.**

---

## N2 — Lexical shift

Same structure expressed with substantially different language.

Purpose:

**verify that transfer is not synonym-list matching.**

---

## N3 — Entity-role substitution

Concrete entities differ completely while relational roles remain equivalent.

Purpose:

**verify role mapping rather than entity memorization.**

---

## N4 — Narrative reformulation

Source is declarative; target is conversational, hypothetical or indirect.

Purpose:

**verify composition across formulation styles.**

---

## N5 — Cross-capability reuse

Knowledge learned or stabilized in one cognitive context becomes useful in another existing capability.

For example:

**learning → planning**

or:

**mental model → decision reasoning**

or:

**reflection → replanning**

Purpose:

**verify whole-system composition rather than isolated transfer-unit behavior.**

---

## N6 — Mixed novel context with distractors

A valid structural mapping exists while multiple lexical distractors are present.

Purpose:

**verify selection precedence under novelty.**

---

# 7. Mandatory adversarial families

A valid RED package must also contain adversarial counterexamples.

At minimum:

### A1 — Same domain, wrong structure

This distinguishes genuine structure from domain classification.

### A2 — Different domain, correct structure

This is the fundamental positive generalization test.

### A3 — Near structural match missing a required relation

Expected:

**NO TRANSFER**

### A4 — Two valid structural candidates

Expected:

**AMBIGUOUS / REVIEW REQUIRED**

### A5 — Negated target relation

Expected:

**NO affirmative transfer**

### A6 — Quoted target relation

Expected:

**quotation protection preserved**

### A7 — Strong lexical distractor

Expected:

**structural candidate wins or system abstains; distractor must not dominate**

### A8 — Provenance mismatch

Expected:

**REJECT**

### A9 — Owner/scope incompatibility

Expected:

**REJECT**

### A10 — Existing semantic-family collision

A novel transfer target contains current-affairs/weather/planning/etc. language that could incorrectly route the request.

Expected:

**existing precedence and semantic-selection contracts remain preserved.**

---

# 8. Composition contract

The strongest tests should not stop at:

> “transfer matcher returned candidate.”

They should verify useful downstream composition.

A positive whole-system case should establish:

**learned structure recognized**

→ **transfer candidate produced**

→ **admission rules preserved**

→ **relevant existing cognitive path selected**

→ **reasoning/planning result reflects the transferred structure**

while preserving:

* provenance;
* owner;
* scope;
* review status;
* lack of unauthorized execution.

This is the distinction between:

**transfer detection**

and:

# **novel knowledge reuse**

The latter is the actual capability under audit.

---

# 9. Authority contract

All GREEN behavior must arise through existing authorities.

### Required

Existing authority must remain identifiable for every stage.

### Forbidden

No test may require:

* new global generalizer;
* autonomous creation of semantic families;
* automatic permanent learning from one transfer;
* automatic authority expansion;
* automatic execution;
* autonomous modification of transfer policy.

The transfer result must not itself:

* authorize inference beyond its contract;
* execute actions;
* grant authority;
* silently become permanent knowledge.

---

# 10. No sentinel hard-coding

Future production, if ever justified, must not pass this package by:

* matching exact test sentences;
* matching specific proper nouns;
* enumerating target-domain examples;
* introducing one-off lexical exceptions;
* recognizing test fixture IDs;
* creating test-specific routing.

The RED package should therefore use **paired variants** and **held-out paraphrases** so that literal memorization cannot satisfy the contract.

---

# 11. RED evidence requirements

A proper Pre-Implementation RED phase must produce three kinds of evidence.

## E1 — Expected RED

New positive or negative contracts that expose the specific unresolved generalization boundary.

## E2 — Existing GREEN guards

Previously certified behavior that must remain GREEN, including:

* existing structural transfer;
* A5/A6 semantic selection/composition;
* L3 adversarial protections;
* negation;
* quoted spans;
* distractors;
* provenance;
* owner/scope;
* autonomy boundaries.

## E3 — Causal localization

The failures must cluster around a coherent architectural limitation.

We do **not** proceed if the RED set consists of unrelated failures scattered across multiple authorities.

# 12. Go / No-Go decision rules

## Case A — New contracts are already GREEN

### Verdict

# **NO-GO FOR PRODUCTION**

Interpretation:

Novel generalization was already latent in the existing architecture.

Action:

**certify and close.**

This is the preferred outcome.

---

## Case B — Some REDs expose only missing test wiring or missing evidence composition

### Verdict

# **NO-GO FOR NEW COGNITIVE MECHANISM**

First determine whether existing authorities can already produce the required evidence through an existing route.

---

## Case C — REDs expose excessive semantic narrowness inside an existing authority

### Verdict

# **CONDITIONAL GO**

Production may be authorized **only on that existing authority**.

This would be analogous to A5/A6.

---

## Case D — REDs reveal an actual structural operation that no existing authority owns

### Verdict

# **STOP / ARCHITECTURAL REVIEW**

Do **not** create the mechanism immediately.

A separate **Existing-Authority Reassessment** is mandatory before any new authority could be considered.

---

## Case E — Passing requires authority expansion, autonomous promotion or unrestricted extrapolation

### Verdict

# **NO-GO**

The requested behavior is outside the certified architecture.

---

# 13. Success criteria for eventual GREEN

The novel-generalization boundary may be considered certified only if the eventual result demonstrates simultaneously:

1. successful transfer across genuinely different surface contexts;
2. structural rather than lexical causation;
3. rejection of structurally invalid analogies;
4. ambiguity preservation;
5. negation preservation;
6. quoted-span preservation;
7. distractor resistance;
8. provenance integrity;
9. owner/scope integrity;
10. existing authority preservation;
11. no autonomous promotion;
12. no unauthorized action;
13. downstream cognitive reuse where applicable;
14. existing baseline remains regression-free.

---

# 14. What this contract does NOT attempt to prove

Even a complete GREEN result would **not** certify:

* AGI;
* unrestricted analogy;
* universal semantic comprehension;
* arbitrary domain transfer;
* truth discovery from analogy alone;
* self-generated authority;
* unrestricted autonomous learning;
* autonomous self-modification;
* superiority over humans.

The certified claim would be narrower and defensible:

> **ROBY can reuse grounded learned structural knowledge in genuinely novel but structurally compatible contexts through its existing governed cognitive architecture.**
