# ADR 225.5 — Conversation History Sanitization

## Status
Accepted.

## Context
Before M225.4, copied observability lines could be persisted as ordinary user turns. The new console boundary prevents future contamination, but historical journal entries remain immutable and may still be selected as recent context.

## Decision
Sanitize the reconstructed, bounded conversation-history view before curation and inference assembly. A complete historical turn is excluded when either its user or assistant text begins with a reserved runtime prefix. The journal itself is never rewritten.

Reserved prefixes are `OBS>`, `ROBY>`, `SYS>`, `TRACE>`, `SYSTEM:`, `ASSISTANT:`, and `USER:` using case-insensitive, trimmed matching.

Observability reports input, sanitized, reserved-removed, and curation-removed turn counts independently.

## Consequences
- Historical contamination cannot reach prompts, structured messages, or providers.
- Auditability is preserved because persisted turns remain unchanged.
- Console sanitization and history sanitization defend separate boundaries.
- Normal domestic conversation, memory, retrieval, tools, and provider routing are unchanged.
