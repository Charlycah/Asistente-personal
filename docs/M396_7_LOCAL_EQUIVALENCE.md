# M396.7 — Local Equivalence Guardrails

## Invariant

**M396-I07 — Local Equivalence**

A change of authorized locality never creates another ROBY. Home, an extended
residence, or a presence endpoint may expose different capabilities and limitations,
but those differences describe the current locality; they do not define a local brain,
identity, personality, memory, learning system, model mental, or cognitive authority.

## Rules

1. `InteractionLocality` identifies where an interaction occurs, not who ROBY is.
2. Residence and endpoint capabilities are contextual facts, not identity facts.
3. Capability asymmetry is valid: local equivalence does not mean hardware equality.
4. Changing from home to an admitted extended residence preserves the same ROBY.
5. A Web endpoint can be an active window without becoming a separate ROBY/residence.
6. Residence Domain defines no local/remote brain, personality, memory or cognitive
   core.
7. Residence Domain imports no cognitive authority and owns no cognitive decision.
8. No replication, synchronization, RPC, transport, remote execution or runtime
   wiring is
   introduced by M396.7.
9. I03 Authority Preservation, I04 Explicit Admission, I05 Stable Residence Identity,
   and I06 Primary Residence Preservation remain binding.

## Non-equivalence deliberately preserved

Local equivalence is semantic continuity, not physical sameness. A cellular residence
may expose sensors unavailable on the laptop; a mini-PC may have different resources;
a Web endpoint may expose another interaction surface. Those facts can inform ROBY's
existing governed cognition later, but cannot instantiate device-specific cognition.

**One ROBY. Different localities. Context changes; identity does not.**
