## 4. Six surviving intellectual horizons

These six horizons survive the historical-roadmap reconciliation. They are **strategic
directions**, not six queued implementation blocks.

### H1 — Deeper Hierarchical Conceptual Intelligence

**Current foundation:** CAP-07 already contains relational abstraction and governed hierarchical
concept composition with productive bounded reachability.

**Surviving horizon:** deepen composition from bounded hierarchical concepts toward richer
multi-concept structures: constituent relations, exceptions, boundaries, parent/child relations,
competing conceptual organizations and component-level provenance across more ordinary
intellectual contexts.

**Reuse first:** CAP-07 `governed_reasoning`; CAP-08 where structural mapping is required.

**Do not create:** ConceptBrain, ontology brain, conceptual memory authority, parallel
abstraction engine.

**Promotion evidence required:** a concrete task that the certified CAP-07/CAP-08 path cannot
represent or consume, with a minimal residual demonstrated in code/tests.

### H2 — Cross-Domain Knowledge Synthesis at Greater Depth

**Current foundation:** CAP-19 is already productively reachable and bounded.

**Surviving horizon:** move from explicit bounded cross-domain correspondence toward deeper
synthesis among concepts, explanatory models, structural transfer, Biblioteca knowledge and
other governed sources while preserving domain/source provenance and uncertainty.

**Reuse first:** CAP-19 + Knowledge + canonical Reasoning + CAP-07/CAP-08 as applicable.

**Do not create:** ROBY Astrónomo, ROBY Biólogo, ROBY Programador or any domain-specific
cognitive brain.

**Promotion evidence required:** a demonstrated synthesis problem not expressible through
existing CAP-19 admission and Reasoning composition.

### H3 — Richer Explanatory Models and Counterfactual Reasoning

**Current foundation:** CAP-05 supports explanatory model composition/revision; CAP-06 supports
advanced counterfactual and multi-stage causal deliberation.

**Surviving horizon:** richer explanatory structures, competing model comparison, longer causal
chains, counterfactual consequences, explanatory adequacy and explicit uncertainty without
promoting hypotheses to facts.

**Reuse first:** CAP-05 and CAP-06 inside `governed_reasoning`.

**Do not create:** TheoryEngine, CounterfactualBrain, causal truth authority.

**Promotion evidence required:** a concrete explanatory/counterfactual task whose required
structure is absent from the existing model and causal contracts.

### H4 — Durable Governed Metacognitive Learning

**Current foundation:** CAP-13 has transient strategy/metacognitive evidence; CAP-12 owns
governed durable Learning.

**Surviving horizon:** allow explicitly reviewed metacognitive evidence to become durable
knowledge about strategy effectiveness, failure conditions, uncertainty handling or method
selection where useful and reversible.

**Reuse first:** CAP-13 evidence → existing CAP-12 Learning authority → existing Memory
materialization path where authorized.

**Do not create:** MetacognitiveMemory, self-rewriting planner, silent durable preference path.

**Promotion evidence required:** a proven useful metacognitive signal, a reversible Learning
contract, provenance, conflict handling and explicit authorization semantics.

## 5. New North-Star evolution candidates

The following ideas are deliberately preserved beyond the six reconciled horizons. They are
**research/evolution candidates only**. They do not become registered capabilities by appearing
here.

### N1 — Temporal World-Model Reasoning

**Intent:** reason not only about facts, but about how facts, entities, relations, projects,
beliefs and evidence change over time.

**Desired intellectual behavior:** distinguish "was true", "is currently supported", "changed",
"became obsolete", and "new evidence explains the transition".

**Reuse hypothesis:** existing World Model / Memory / longitudinal reasoning / epistemic
assessment. First determine which authority already owns each temporal claim before proposing
any new representation.

**Guardrail:** temporal reconstruction must not rewrite historical evidence or silently turn
recalled state into current truth.

### N2 — Epistemic Conflict Resolution

**Intent:** explicitly represent and reason over conflicts among Biblioteca, personal documents,
external/acquired knowledge, Memory and ROBY's prior inferences.

**Desired intellectual behavior:** preserve provenance, recency, confidence, source independence
and unresolved disagreement; identify what evidence could discriminate among competing claims.

**Reuse hypothesis:** CAP-05, CAP-09, existing conflict analysis, Knowledge/Memory provenance
and CAP-11 evidence acquisition.

**Guardrail:** conflict resolution is not majority voting and does not create a new truth
authority.

### N3 — Governed Theory Building

**Intent:** maintain coherent explanatory structures whose principles can be strengthened,
weakened or revised by later evidence.

**Desired intellectual behavior:** relate multiple hypotheses and explanatory models without
presenting a theory as established fact merely because it is internally coherent.

**Reuse hypothesis:** CAP-05 explanatory models + CAP-06 causal reasoning + CAP-07 conceptual
hierarchy + CAP-19 cross-domain synthesis.

**Guardrail:** no standalone TheoryEngine unless exhaustive reuse proof demonstrates an unowned
residual.

### N4 — Cognitive Experimentation

**Intent:** when several explanations or strategies remain plausible, formulate small governed
tests that could discriminate among them.

**Desired intellectual behavior:** propose experiment → predict discriminating observations →
request/obtain authorization where action is needed → evaluate evidence → update the existing
model.

**Reuse hypothesis:** CAP-05 discrimination, CAP-11 investigation,
Planning/Authorization/Execution boundaries, CAP-13 strategy evidence.

**Guardrail:** proposing an experiment never grants permission to execute it.

### N5 — Knowledge Compression and Conceptual Distillation

**Intent:** allow long-term accumulated knowledge to be summarized into higher-order concepts
without losing traceability to constituent evidence.

**Desired intellectual behavior:** compress many observations into reusable conceptual structure
while retaining provenance, exceptions, uncertainty and the ability to reopen supporting
evidence.

**Reuse hypothesis:** CAP-07 hierarchical concepts + Knowledge + CAP-12 Learning + Memory
provenance.

**Guardrail:** compression must not erase contradictory evidence, source identity or
uncertainty.

### N6 — Intellectual Reconsideration

**Intent:** detect when newly admitted knowledge materially affects an older conclusion, model
or open question and propose reconsideration.

**Desired intellectual behavior:** "this earlier conclusion depends on a premise now
contradicted or weakened; review is warranted."

**Reuse hypothesis:** CAP-05 model revision + CAP-09 epistemic assessment + longitudinal
continuity + existing governed evidence paths.

**Guardrail:** detection may create a review candidate; it must not silently rewrite durable
knowledge or historical conclusions.

### N7 — Cognitive Portfolio / Open Questions

**Intent:** maintain a governed view of major unresolved questions, competing hypotheses,
contradictions, suspended investigations and knowledge gaps worth revisiting.

**Desired intellectual behavior:** preserve intellectual continuity without requiring continuous
background execution.

**Reuse hypothesis:** existing Cognitive Agenda + CAP-10 Curiosity + CAP-11 investigation
lifecycle + CAP-13 metacognitive evidence.

**Guardrail:** extend the existing Cognitive Agenda only if evidence proves the needed semantics
belong there; do not create a second agenda, task brain or autonomous background loop.

## 6. Strategic convergence model

The long-range intellectual direction is not "more modules" and not "maximum autonomy". The
desired convergence is:

**observe / retrieve → understand → relate → abstract → explain → discriminate →**  
**investigate → learn when authorized → remember with provenance → reconsider →**  
**transfer →**  
**self-assess → propose improvement**

inside **one governed cognitive architecture**.

A mature ROBY should progressively become better at:

- solving unfamiliar problems through reusable conceptual structure;
- connecting knowledge across domains and modalities;
- constructing and revising explanations;
- distinguishing evidence, inference, hypothesis, uncertainty and ignorance;
- maintaining intellectual continuity across long periods;
- learning only through governed durable paths;
- recognizing when previous conclusions deserve review;
- explaining its real capabilities and limitations;
- proposing improvements without acquiring self-promotion authority;
- expressing authorized content with a coherent identity without turning expression into
  cognition.

This is the intended meaning of deeper intelligence in this roadmap.
