## Purpose

Preserve the future research direction raised after the governed neural benchmark
without creating a parallel ethics engine, reasoning authority, planner, learning
store, or neural brain. This reconciliation classifies the proposed ideas against
ROBY's current canonical architecture before any implementation is authorized.

This document does not claim consciousness, subjective morality, feelings, or
human-like moral agency. The target is functional, inspectable deliberation over
options, consequences, uncertainty, existing principles, permissions, and human
authority.

## Reconciled inventory

### Logical and inferential reasoning — EXISTING / DUPLICATE-RISK

CAP-04 already defines `governed_reasoning` as the canonical inferential owner.
Existing governed reasoning includes rules, hypotheses, conflicts, inference,
explanations, derived assessments, reasoning-quality assessment, and method
awareness. A new LogicEngine, ReasoningManager, or parallel reasoning brain is
therefore forbidden unless a later reconciliation proves a genuinely absent
primitive that cannot extend the canonical authority.

### Scenario, causal and counterfactual reasoning — EXISTING / PARTIAL

CAP-06 already contains scenario simulation, causal prediction, governed
prediction, outcome evaluation, multi-stage causal paths, and counterfactual path
comparison. The documented residual is deeper multi-stage counterfactual work,
not creation of another causal engine.

### Uncertainty, epistemic sufficiency and abstention — EXISTING

ROBY already represents confidence/uncertainty and governed epistemic sufficiency.
Existing behavioral principles require distinction between fact, inference,
hypothesis, and unknown, plus safe abstention when evidence or authority is
insufficient. A separate uncertainty authority is unnecessary.

### Human autonomy, permission and consent — EXISTING

Existing permission/consent boundaries, planning contracts, and behavioral
principles preserve human authority and prevent capability availability from being
interpreted as permission. Sensitive action remains review/consent governed.

### Behavioral and safety principles — EXISTING / DUPLICATE-RISK

`core/cognitive_maturity/behavior.py` already defines explicit principles covering
human authority, prudent care without overreach, epistemic honesty, privacy and
minimization, consent and least privilege, bounded/reversible action, prohibition
of self-authorization, non-manipulative relationship, perceptual truthfulness,
governed memory/learning, safe explainable degradation, and model-independent
identity.

It also defines a conflict policy: principles do not grant action authority,
specialized domain authority remains authoritative, unresolved conflict requires
safe abstention, and the human owners retain review/correction/revocation.

Therefore a new moral catalogue, EthicsManager, or autonomous moral authority
would duplicate existing architecture and is not authorized.

### Explicit decision alternatives and deliberation intake — EXISTING / PARTIAL

ROBY already detects explicit human decision alternatives through a deterministic,
read-only evidence producer. It deliberately does not generate alternatives, rank
them, execute actions, persist conclusions, or grant authority. Future work must
extend governed decision/reasoning paths rather than replace this boundary.

### Outcome-based experience and metacognition — EXISTING / PARTIAL

Prediction outcome evaluation, longitudinal reasoning observation, method outcome
association, reasoning-quality assessment, historical method advisory, governed
Learning, and Memory provide substantial foundations for learning from outcomes.
Durable behavioral adaptation remains a Learning decision and must never become
silent weight, rule, permission, or memory mutation.

### Neural semantic assistance — EXISTING SEAM / MODEL UNSELECTED

The contextual-memory neural reranking seam is advisory only and its benchmark is
Owner-certified. Neural components may later contribute bounded evidence such as
semantic relevance or risk cues, but may not become a moral authority, execution
authority, second Reasoning system, or hidden policy source.

## Exact future residual

The strongest non-duplicative candidate is:

`GOVERNED MULTI-PRINCIPLE NORMATIVE DELIBERATION`

This is not a new authority. It is a candidate governed reasoning/decision
capability that would consume already-authorized evidence and existing behavioral
principles to make tensions explicit.

Candidate responsibilities:

1. identify which existing principles are materially relevant to an explicit
   decision or proposed action;
2. represent conflicts or tensions without silently inventing a hierarchy;
3. associate explicit/derived consequences and uncertainty with the alternatives;
4. distinguish hard authority/permission constraints from advisory normative
   considerations and from personal preferences;
5. assess proportionality and reversibility when those properties are supported
   by governed evidence;
6. expose unresolved conflicts and uncertainty rather than manufacture certainty;
7. produce an explainable advisory comparison for existing decision/planning
   authorities;
8. preserve human review, correction, consent, and revocation;
9. never execute, authorize, persist learning, or change principles by itself.

## Freedom-preserving rule

Ethical deliberation must not collapse into paternalistic prohibition. A future
contract must distinguish at least:

- an action forbidden or unavailable by an existing hard authority;
- an action requiring explicit permission or consent;
- an action that is permitted but has material risks or consequences;
- an action involving a personal value choice where ROBY should inform rather
  than substitute its preference;
- an unresolved case where abstention or clarification is required.

The user remains the decision authority wherever no higher existing legal,
safety, permission, or domain boundary applies.

## Candidate future research sequence

The candidate must follow the normal governed workflow and must not be implemented
from this document alone:

1. pre-RED reconciliation of productive callers and existing decision/reasoning
   seams;
2. contract design for normative evidence, conflict representation, provenance,
   uncertainty, proportionality, reversibility, and explanation;
3. contractual RED with synthetic, non-private dilemmas and explicit anti-
   paternalism cases;
4. surgical GREEN inside existing authorities only;
5. Owner certification and post-GREEN reconciliation;
6. only later, evaluate whether any neural specialist adds measurable value as
   advisory evidence.

## Explicitly forbidden shortcuts

- `EthicsManager`, `MoralManager`, or second reasoning authority;
- a neural model deciding whether an action is morally permitted;
- hardcoded universal moral ranking that silently overrides human authority;
- using confidence as truth or moral correctness;
- autonomous modification of principles, permissions, code, or model weights;
- direct execution from normative or neural evidence;
- silent durable learning from one decision outcome;
- claims of consciousness, feelings, moral personhood, or subjective experience.

## Research questions preserved for future increments

- How should conflicts among existing principles be represented without inventing
  an unjustified total ordering?
- How should consequences, uncertainty, reversibility, and proportionality be
  combined while preserving provenance?
- When should ROBY recommend, warn, request clarification, abstain, or defer to a
  human decision?
- How can outcome evidence improve later deliberation through governed Learning
  without silently rewriting behavior?
- How should Roby explain trade-offs in natural language without presenting an
  advisory assessment as objective moral truth?
- Can a bounded neural specialist improve recognition of semantically implicit
  risks or relevant principles while deterministic authorities retain control?
