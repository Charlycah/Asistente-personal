## 3. Need-to-ask semantics

For the currently certified S5 lineage, `NEED_TO_ASK` is established only by
the conjunction of:

- an actual governed self-epistemic selection lineage;
- an actual canonical Authorization outcome preserved through productive
  consumption;
- B1's certified `NO_COMPATIBLE_CANONICAL_ROUTE` determination;
- no acquisition or execution authorization; and
- no fabricated fallback route or synthetic Planning lineage.

`NEED_TO_ASK` means only that the unresolved self-epistemic need may proceed to
a later governed question-worthiness boundary.

It does not mean `ASK_NOW`, does not identify a human, and does not create a
question candidate.

The Authorization decision is preserved exactly but is not translated.

`MUST_ASK != NEED_TO_ASK BY ITSELF`

`MUST_ASK != ASK NOW`

`MAY_INITIATE != NO NEED TO ASK`

No Authorization enum value alone may determine C1. C1 depends on the exact
certified reachability facts as well as the preserved self-epistemic lineage.
