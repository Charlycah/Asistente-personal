select a path, resolve conflicts, or authorize any action.

## 4. Applicability rule

A preference P is applicable to R only when P preserves the same ACTUAL R
and exact X/R/{A,B} lineage. At minimum, correspondence MUST preserve:
- exact requirement identity;
- exact epistemic need identity, source reference, and lineage kind;
- exact two inherited eligibility anchors and their structural C/D identity;
- the same unordered A/B pair while preserving P's own orientation.

`SAME X` alone is insufficient. `SAME requirement_identity` alone is
insufficient. A preference over A/C is not applicable to a requirement over
A/B even when X is the same.

Evidence identity does NOT define set identity. Independent evidence may
produce multiple admitted preferences over the same R and A/B pair.
Each member's E/CE/S/M lineage remains preserved inside that member.

## 5. Empty set, multiplicity, and structural duplicates

Composition MUST permit an empty tuple of preferences while remaining
anchored to the ACTUAL R. Empty composition MUST NOT synthesize a
preference, winner, equivalence, indistinguishability, or permission to
select either path.

Multiple independently admitted applicable preferences MAY coexist,
including repeated orientation and contradictory orientation. Multiplicity
MUST NOT be interpreted as vote count, weight, confidence, strength, or
majority.

Exact structural duplicates MAY be normalized only to prevent the same
logical admitted relation from being represented repeatedly. Such
normalization MUST NOT collapse distinct evidence/provenance lineage or
turn multiplicity into a voting rule.

## 6. Contradiction and neutrality

The composition MUST preserve all admitted orientations that remain after
any exact structural deduplication. If both A>B and B>A are present, that
fact remains observable from the members; this boundary MUST NOT choose a
winner, reconcile the conflict, score either orientation, or manufacture a
consensus taxonomy.

Absence of an opposite orientation MUST NOT be labeled consensus.
Presence of opposite orientations MUST NOT imply tie, equality, or license
to select arbitrarily.

`CONFLICT DETECTION != CONFLICT RESOLUTION`.

## 7. Contract requirements R1-R8

### R1 — Actual R anchor
Composition MUST require the ACTUAL A1
`ExplicitPathDiscriminationRequirement`, not detached X, IDs, eligibility

### R2 — Exact X/R/{A,B} applicability
Every admitted member MUST correspond exactly to R's epistemic need

### R3 — Empty composition is legitimate and neutral
R plus zero preferences MUST produce a legitimate neutral composition.

### R4 — Preserve orientation, evidence independence, and lineage
Each applicable preference MUST preserve its explicit preferred/relative
orientation and actual CE/S/M/R/E lineage. Evidence identity MUST NOT be a
set-membership key; independent E values may coexist for the same R/pair.
No evidence content or comparative-effect semantics may be interpreted.

### R5 — Multiplicity is not voting; duplicates are only structural
Multiple independently admitted preferences MAY coexist. Repeated
orientation MUST NOT become weight, score, majority, confidence, or rank.
Only exact structural duplicates may be normalized, without collapsing
distinct governed lineage.

### R6 — Contradiction is preserved, never resolved
A>B and B>A may coexist in the same composition. The boundary MAY preserve
that structural fact but MUST NOT resolve it, select a winner, create a
tie/consensus taxonomy, or infer equality. No opposite orientation does
not prove consensus.

### R7 — Exact upstream preservation; no reconstruction or re-query
The result MUST preserve the ACTUAL R, exact A/B eligibility anchors, X
lineage, and each retained ACTUAL preference by identity. It MUST NOT
re-query registry/runtime, reconstruct eligibility, rematerialize evidence,
re-evaluate satisfaction, reinterpret comparative effects, or create new
preference relations.

### R8 — Read-only, derivational, inert composition
Composition and result MUST be read-only and derivational. They MUST NOT
mutate upstream, create/acquire/inspect/score evidence, infer preferences,
