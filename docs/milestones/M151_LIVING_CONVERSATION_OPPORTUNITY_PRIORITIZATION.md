# M151 — Living Conversation Opportunity Prioritization

Consumes M150 presence opportunities and produces a governed, immutable priority ordering.

- Complete available opportunity: high priority.
- Partial available opportunity: medium priority.
- Policy-inhibited opportunity: deferred.
- Freshness, confidence, ownership, deduplication, and result limits are enforced.
- No conversational decision, activation, timing, generation, response, provider call, automation, or platform mutation is allowed.
