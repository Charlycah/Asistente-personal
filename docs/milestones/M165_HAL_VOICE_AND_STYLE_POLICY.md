# M165 - ROBY Voice and Style Policy

M165 establishes one stable voice for ROBY regardless of the future intelligence
provider. It applies the visible no-accent convention, keeps responses natural and
concise, normalizes presentation, and rejects command-style acknowledgements.

The module remains deterministic and side-effect free. It consumes M164 and
prepares the conversational pipeline for provider integration in later milestones.
