# M213 — Fault Recovery Coordination

M213 converts the M212 degradation decision into a deterministic recovery plan. The coordinator verifies healthy baselines, observes conservative restrictions, coordinates bounded retries during essential operation, and holds restricted capabilities during containment.

The milestone preserves exact lineage through M212, M211, and M210; publishes the effective recovery state to the unified cognitive blackboard; keeps recovery steps contiguous and traceable; and guarantees that conversation continuity is not held by recovery policy.

The coordinator owns recovery sequencing only. It cannot restart providers, invoke tools, modify infrastructure, mutate platform configuration, bypass graceful degradation, or certify long-running stability. Those authorities remain outside M213.
