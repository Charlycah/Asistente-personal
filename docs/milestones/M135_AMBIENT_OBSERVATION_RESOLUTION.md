# M135 — Ambient Observation Resolution

M135 resolves which governed representation of an M134 ambient observation may continue through the Ambient Context pipeline.

## Responsibility
Select a declarative representation for a declared purpose while preserving exact M134 lineage, consent, privacy, confidence, and provenance governance.

## Output
`ResolvedAmbientObservation` records resolution status, selected candidate, future understanding requirements, rationale, and deterministic identity.

## Explicit exclusions
No sensor access, presence or activity inference, acoustic analysis, environmental-condition analysis, spatial inference, automation, provider calls, or platform mutation.
