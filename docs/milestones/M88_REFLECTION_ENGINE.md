# M88 — Reflection Engine

M88 introduces governed longitudinal reflection over M87 assessments. It detects
score trends, recurring severe dimensions, repeated recommendations and review
frequency. Results are immutable, traceable and advisory.

## Boundaries

M88 does not rewrite episodes, mental models, reasoning results or decisions. It
does not promote knowledge, modify policy, execute recommendations or authorize
actions. M49 remains the operational reflection component of the legacy cognitive
cycle; M88 is the Phase IX adaptive reflection layer.

## Integration

The Cognitive Pipeline can optionally run M88 after M87. A single-turn record is
marked `insufficient`; longitudinal callers can provide multiple assessments for
stable, improving, degrading or mixed outcomes.
