# M212 — Graceful Degradation Engine

M212 converts the M211 resilience posture into a deterministic and reversible cognitive service mode. It preserves conversation continuity in every mode, progressively limits nonessential capabilities, publishes the effective mode to the unified cognitive blackboard, and preserves complete assessment lineage.

The engine owns degradation policy only. It cannot repair faults, retry failed work, restore components, coordinate recovery, change providers, invoke tools, or mutate platform configuration. Those responsibilities remain outside M212 and fault recovery coordination begins in M213.
