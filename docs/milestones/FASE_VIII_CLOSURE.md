ROBY can now reason over Personal Operations information through a deterministic and traceable chain that preserves source provenance, separates facts from inferences, detects contradictions, quantifies bounded confidence, records decisions and produces public explanations.

## Preserved boundaries

- Scope Guard remains authoritative.
- Human review remains explicit.
- Reasoning does not authorize action.
- No external sensitive action occurs without the established autonomy and permission layers.
- Inferences are not silently converted into facts.
- Audit records contain public reasons and references, not unrestricted chain-of-thought.
