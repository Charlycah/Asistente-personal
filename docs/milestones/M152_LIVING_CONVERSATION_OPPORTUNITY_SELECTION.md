# M152 — Living Conversation Opportunity Selection

M152 consumes governed M151 prioritization results and selects one declarative opportunity candidate.

- High priority outranks medium and deferred.
- Confidence, recency, and deterministic identifiers resolve ties.
- Partial selection remains explicit and configurable.
- Deferred selection remains non-activating.
- Source prioritization and opportunity objects are preserved exactly.
- No conversation decision, timing, prompt generation, response realization, automation, or platform mutation is allowed.
