# M104 — Intellectual Curiosity Engine

## Objective
Transform M103 shared intellectual interests into governed, ranked possibilities for further inquiry while preserving strict separation from activation, timing, realization, memory, and learning.

## Delivered

- curiosity kinds: question, connection, counterpoint, exploration, revisit;
- immutable request, prompt, plan, policy, repository, and engine contracts;
- source-preserving prompt derivation;
- score bonuses for explicit, established, core, and connected interests;
- exclusions and requested-kind filtering;
- configurable prompt limit and counterpoint policy;
- conservative review and non-applicable propagation;
- rhythm-aware rationale without scheduling or delivery;
- deterministic plan identifiers;
- in-memory append-only repository;
- public exports through `core.living_conversation`;
- 40 focused tests.

## Architectural boundary
M104 proposes directions of curiosity. It does not speak, activate, schedule, remember, learn, or modify prior FASE X outputs.

## Validation

- Ruff: clean
- Pytest: 1560 passed
- M104 tests: 40 passed
- Regressions: none
